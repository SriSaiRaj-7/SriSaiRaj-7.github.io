Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)

RaSoR Club, IIT Madras BS — Active ideation / early-stage development

Models that embed physical laws — differential equations — directly into the neural network's loss function, so predictions respect the underlying physics.

  • Studying how PDE residuals become loss terms and combine with data-driven training, building on linear algebra and calculus.
  • Practicing the full research workflow: reading primary literature, breaking papers into first principles, shaping an original direction within a collaborative subgroup.

REEL II · TEAM LEAD · EARLY-STAGE DEVELOPMENT

NotebookLM-Style Document Chatbot (RAG)

B.Tech Team Project, SVCT — Team Lead, 4 members

A personalized, local chatbot that parses uploaded PDFs and answers questions grounded in their content — in the style of Google's NotebookLM.

  • Own the backend and RAG pipeline: document parsing, retrieval, LLM prompting — plus the research design, with a focus on evaluating answer faithfulness to source documents.
  • Leading a four-person team on architecture and planned features (PPT generation, short-note PDFs, question-paper generation) — sharpening system design, prompt engineering, and collaboration.
